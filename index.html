Wellness Wonders Uploader

Wellness Wonders Uploader is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Wellness Wonders YouTube channel. The channel publishes short, fact-checked videos that explain everyday health and wellness questions, such as what happens in your body when you try a popular trend.

What the app does

  • Uploads short videos made by the channel owner to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Sets each video's title, description, tags and category.
  • Checks that an upload finished processing and removes accidental duplicate copies of that same upload.
  • Sets a video's visibility (unlisted, private, scheduled or public) when the owner approves it.

The app works only with the owner's own channel. It is not offered to the public and no other people sign in to it.
