Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026 · Last updated: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Wellness Wonders Uploader ("the app") accesses, how it is used, stored and shared, and how access can be removed. The app is operated by the owner of the Wellness Wonders YouTube channel and is used only to manage that channel.

1. Who uses the app

The only user of the app is the owner of the Wellness Wonders YouTube channel. The app is not available to the public, does not have user accounts, and does not collect information from viewers, subscribers or any other person.

2. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services to work with the owner's YouTube channel. By using the app, the user agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Information the app accesses and collects

When the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app requests these OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload: to upload videos to the owner's channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube: to read and update the owner's own videos.

With these permissions the app accesses only the following information about the owner's own channel:

  • Authorization data: an OAuth access token and refresh token issued by Google.
  • Channel identity: the channel ID and channel name, to confirm the correct channel is connected.
  • Video metadata for the owner's own uploads: video ID, title, description, tags, category, upload and processing status, duration, privacy status and scheduled publish time.
  • Video files: the video files the owner created and chose to upload.

The app does not access or collect comments, viewer or subscriber information, YouTube Analytics or reporting data, watch history, playlists of other users, payment information, contacts, or any data from any other YouTube account.

4. How the information is used

  • To upload the owner's videos to the owner's channel with the title, description, tags and category the owner chose.
  • To check that each upload finished processing and to delete accidental duplicate or broken copies of that same upload.
  • To set a video's visibility (unlisted, private, scheduled or public) when the owner approves it.
  • To keep a private log of the owner's uploads (date, topic, title, video link and status).

The information is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, training AI models or any purpose other than those listed above.

5. How the information is stored and protected

  • The OAuth refresh token and app credentials are stored privately in the owner's own account settings for the automation and are accessible only to the owner. They are transmitted only over encrypted HTTPS connections to Google.
  • Access tokens are short-lived (about one hour) and are not stored long term.
  • Video files are kept only in a temporary working environment for as long as needed to upload them and are then discarded.
  • The upload log (video IDs, titles, links and statuses) is kept in the owner's private Google Sheet.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Authorization data is kept until the owner revokes access. After revocation, the stored token stops working and is deleted from the automation settings.
  • Video metadata in the upload log is kept for as long as the owner wants a record of uploads and can be deleted by the owner at any time.
  • Videos on YouTube can be deleted by the owner at any time in YouTube Studio.
  • Any information obtained through YouTube API Services that is stored by the app is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies, except where the owner keeps it in their own records.

7. Sharing and disclosure

The app does not sell, rent, trade or share any information with third parties. Information is sent only to Google/YouTube to perform the actions described above. Video files may pass through a temporary file transfer service solely to deliver the owner's own video to YouTube; these temporary copies expire automatically within hours.

8. Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access to their Google account and YouTube channel at any time on the Google security settings page (Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access → Wellness Wonders Uploader → Remove access).

9. Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

10. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not collect information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
